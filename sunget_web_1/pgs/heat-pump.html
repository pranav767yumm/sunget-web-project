How a heat pump heats your water

It works like a refrigerator in reverse: it collects heat from the air and puts it in the tank.

  1. Takes heat from the air

    A fan draws air over a coil. A refrigerant inside absorbs the heat, even when the air feels cool.

  2. Compresses it

    A compressor raises the temperature of the refrigerant. This step is the only part that uses much electricity.

  3. Warms the water

    The hot refrigerant passes its heat to the water in an insulated storage tank, ready for use.

How is a heat pump different from a solar water heater?

A solar heater needs sunshine. A heat pump uses heat from the air, so it works at night and in the monsoon. Many customers combine both.

Does it work in cool weather?

Yes. Pune's climate is well suited to heat pumps. Efficiency dips slightly in the coldest weeks, but it still uses far less power than an electric heater.

How much space does it need?

The outdoor unit needs open, ventilated space such as a terrace, balcony or yard. It also needs room for a storage tank. We check this on the site visit.

Is it noisy?

It makes a low hum similar to an air conditioner outdoor unit. We position it away from bedrooms and seating areas.

Can I run it on solar power?

Yes. A rooftop solar system can supply the heat pump's electricity during the day, which brings your hot water cost close to zero.

How long does it last?

A well-maintained unit commonly runs 10–15 years or more. The compressor is the main component, and it is covered by the manufacturer's warranty.
