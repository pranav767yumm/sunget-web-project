Where businesses lose money on power

Most of it is invisible on the bill. An audit makes it visible, and fixable.

Poor power factor

A low power factor can attract penalties and wastes network capacity.

High peak demand

A short spike sets your demand charge for the whole month. Many spikes can be avoided.

Harmonics and imbalance

Electronic loads and uneven phases heat cables and motors and shorten equipment life.

Wrong tariff or contract demand

Paying for more contract demand than you use, or missing time-of-day benefits.

What the audit covers

Four parts that build on each other, from measurement to a solar plan.

Power quality assessment

A logging analyser is connected to your main incomer and key feeders for several days. It records voltage, current, power factor, harmonics and disturbances, so we see what really happens at your site, not just a single reading.

Best for: sites with equipment trips, overheating, penalties or unexplained losses.

Load audit

We list your major loads, measure how much each draws and when, and build a load profile through the day. This shows what can be switched off, shifted, resized or replaced with a more efficient option.

Tariff and contract optimisation

We study 12 months of bills against your actual demand and usage. We check contract demand, power factor charges, time-of-day rates and the category you are billed under, and tell you what to change.

How an audit works

From first call to a plan you can act on, with minimal disturbance to your operations.

  1. Scoping call

    We understand your site, supply, bills and what you want from the audit.

  2. Data collection

    Single line diagram, 12 months of bills, equipment lists and operating schedules.

  3. Site measurement

    Walk-through, spot readings and installation of logging analysers.

  4. Analysis

    Our engineers study the data, find losses and calculate savings for each fix.

  5. Report and review

    A clear report with priorities, cost, savings and payback, walked through with your team.

  6. Implementation support

    Optional help with the fixes, including solar, capacitors, filters and controls.

Your audit report includes

  • Measured data with graphs and findings
  • Load profile and breakdown by area
  • Tariff and contract demand review
  • Ranked list of actions with cost and payback
  • Solar sizing and generation estimate
  • Summary for management and engineers

Questions about energy audits

Not here? Call +91 98818 55770 and we will answer.

Will the audit disturb my operations?

Very little. Most measurements are taken with clamp-on instruments while you run normally. Any connection that needs a short shutdown is planned with your team in advance.

How long does an audit take?

A walk-through can be done in a day. A detailed audit usually needs a few days of logging plus time for analysis and the report. We confirm the schedule when we scope it.

What do you need from me?

Your last 12 months of electricity bills, a single line diagram if you have one, a list of major equipment and access to the electrical panels.

Do I have to buy solar or anything else from you?

No. The report is yours, and you can act on it with any vendor. We are happy to help with implementation if you want us to.

Is this a statutory or mandatory energy audit?

Some large industrial consumers must have audits done by an accredited energy auditor. Tell us your requirement and we will confirm whether our audit meets it before you commit.

How do I know the savings are real?

Each recommendation shows the measurement it is based on, the calculation and the assumptions. After implementation, you can compare bills before and after to confirm the result.
