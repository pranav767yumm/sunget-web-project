How a CSR project runs

A clear sequence that keeps funders informed and communities involved.

  1. Understand goals

    We learn your objectives, budget, timeline and the kind of impact you want to show.

  2. Choose beneficiary and site

    We shortlist places and visit them, speaking with the people who will use the system.

  3. Plan and approvals

    Design, budget, permissions and an agreed schedule, shared with you for sign-off.

  4. Execute

    Supply and installation by our crew, with regular updates and photos for your team.

  5. Train and hand over

    Local caretakers are trained, and the system is formally handed to the beneficiary.

  6. Report impact

    A report with generation, CO₂ avoided, beneficiaries and photos, ready for your CSR records.

Every CSR project includes

  • Site survey and community consultation
  • Design, supply and installation
  • Training for local caretakers
  • A maintenance period after handover
  • Photo documentation at each stage
  • Impact report with energy and CO₂ figures

Does solar qualify as CSR spending?

Many companies in India are required to spend on CSR under the Companies Act, and environmental sustainability and rural development are among the areas it lists. Please confirm eligibility for your project with your CSR committee and advisers.

How do you choose the beneficiary?

Either you tell us the school, village or institution you want to support, or we shortlist options that match your goals and visit them before you decide.

What happens after the system is installed?

Local caretakers are trained, and we provide a maintenance period after handover. We can also offer a longer maintenance plan so the system keeps working for years.

How do you report the results?

You receive a report covering what was installed, who benefits, how much energy it produces and the CO₂ avoided, with photos from each stage.

Can you run a programme for a small budget?

Yes. Awareness workshops and small installations such as solar street lights or a single school plant can be scoped to your budget. Share the figure and we will propose what fits.

Can we involve our employees?

Yes. We can plan a volunteering day around the installation or a workshop, so your team sees the result of the project first-hand.
