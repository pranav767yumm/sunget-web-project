Choose the right type of system

Pick by what happens when the grid is off and whether you want to store power.

On-grid (grid-tied)

Panels feed your building first. Extra units go to the grid and are credited on your bill through net-metering. No batteries, so it costs less and needs almost no upkeep.

Best for: most homes, societies, offices and factories with a stable grid.

Hybrid

Questions people ask before going solar

Not here? Call +91 98818 55770 and we will answer.

Will solar work on cloudy or monsoon days?

Yes, but output drops. Pune gets strong sun for most of the year, and the yearly total is what decides your savings, not any single day.

How much roof space do I need?

Plan for roughly 100 sq ft of shadow-free area for each kW. A 3 kW home system needs about 300 sq ft.

What is net-metering?

A bi-directional meter records the power you send to the grid and the power you draw. The difference is what you pay for.

Will my system work during a power cut?

An on-grid system shuts off during an outage for lineman safety. If you want backup, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

How long do solar panels last?

Good panels are warrantied for 25 years or more and keep producing after that at lower output. Inverters usually need replacing once in that time.

How much maintenance does it need?

Mostly cleaning the panels every few weeks and an annual check of wiring and the inverter. We offer service plans if you prefer us to handle it.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Easy EMI options are available for home systems, and commercial customers can choose between capex and opex models.
