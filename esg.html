How an ESG energy project runs

A clear sequence from baseline to reported results.

  1. Understand targets

    We learn your ESG goals, reporting framework and timelines.

  2. Baseline

    Bills, load profile and current emissions from electricity and fuel.

  3. Reduce waste

    An energy audit finds quick efficiency wins before solar is sized.

  4. Design and approvals

    Solar plant design, DISCOM approvals and net-metering handled by us.

  5. Build

    Installation, testing and commissioning by our crew.

  6. Monitor and report

    Generation data and CO₂-avoided figures shared on a regular schedule.

Questions about ESG and solar

Not here? Call +91 98818 55770 and we will answer.

Do you prepare our ESG or BRSR report?

No. We supply the energy data, generation figures and documents. Your sustainability team or auditor prepares and signs off the report.

Which emissions does solar reduce?

Mainly Scope 2, which comes from purchased grid electricity. Solar water heating and heat pumps can also reduce fuel-related Scope 1 emissions.

Can we claim renewable energy certificates?

Rules for certificates and carbon claims depend on your plant type and framework. We explain what applies to your case when we quote.

Should we do an audit before solar?

It is a good idea. Cutting waste first can reduce the solar size you need and improve payback.

Can you cover several of our sites?

Yes. We can survey and plan multiple sites together and give you one consolidated report.

Can this link to our CSR spending?

Where eligible, projects such as school or village solar can be planned as CSR. Please confirm eligibility with your CSR committee and advisers.
