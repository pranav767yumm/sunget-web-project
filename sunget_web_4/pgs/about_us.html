Our story

By the people, for the people.

SunGET Solar Infra is a Pune-based solar company. We began with a simple belief: switching to solar should feel straightforward, honest and worth it. Today we deliver rooftop systems, solar water heating, heat pumps, turnkey EPC, maintenance and energy audits across Maharashtra.

Our working philosophy is to measure first, design properly, install safely, and stay after the handover. Our team brings engineers, project managers, consultants and service technicians together, so customers deal with one partner from the first survey to the long-term service.

Our Mission

To create a greener and cleaner India by promoting solar energy and making renewable power accessible to every household and business. We envision a sustainable future powered by clean, reliable, and affordable solar energy.

Our Vision

To deliver high-quality, reliable, and affordable solar energy solutions through expert engineering, innovative technology, and professional EPC services. We aim to spread awareness about renewable energy and empower every household to generate its own clean electricity.
